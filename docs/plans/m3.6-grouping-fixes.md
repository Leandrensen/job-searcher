# M3.6: Review fixes for the M3.5 grouping and seniority changes

Base: commit `560ad62` on branch `fix/m3.5-pre-m4`. Executed with the `execute-plan` skill.

## Goal
Fix what the review of M3.5 found, before that branch is pushed:
1. The job filter rules are checked before copies are grouped, so a passing copy is ranked even when the lowest-id copy fails.
2. Copies linked only through a skipped copy are still recognized as the same posting.
3. On one job board, the same company and title only counts as one posting when the descriptions are identical too.
4. Seniority has a single `mid` rule that also understands "SSr", "semisenior" and "semi-senior".
5. Rank prints a helpful message when every open job fails the rules.

- Decisions already made:
  - **Same posting.** Two open jobs are the same posting if any of these hold:
    - they share a `duplicateOf` family;
    - they share a non-null `applyUrl`;
    - they share `dedupeKey` (company + title) **and** come from different sources;
    - they share `dedupeKey`, come from the same source **and** have identical `descriptionText`.

    Why: on one board, the same title with different text is a different opening (in the live DB, Veeva has two "Software Engineer in Test" jobs with different descriptions). Across sources, the text is formatted differently, so title + company is enough, as in `findOriginal`.
  - **Every seen row counts for grouping, kept or skipped**, so chains of copies collapse to one.
  - **Rows that fail the rules are left out before grouping:** they are neither kept nor remembered.
  - **Seniority:**
    - One `mid` rule, placed after `senior`.
    - Any level word right after `"semi "` doesn't count, so "Semi Senior" is mid, not senior.
    - Rule words get the same normalization as titles, so `"semi-senior"` and `"mid-level"` are written naturally.
    - A title naming several levels gets the highest one, by rule order: "Sr/SSr" and "Senior / Semi Senior" are senior, while "Junior/Semi-Senior" is mid. This replaces M3.5's untested statement that a title with both is mid.
  - **"ssr" is accepted as semi senior,** although it also matches "SSR" (server-side rendering) in a title with no other level. That case is pinned by a test as a known false positive.

## Gotchas
- **Normalize the rule words** with the same `normalize` as titles. Otherwise `"semi-senior"` never matches, because titles have their hyphens turned into spaces.
- **The `(?<!semi )` guard goes in every pattern.** It's harmless for levels other than senior.
- **Strip the grouping fields:** `source`, `duplicateOf`, `dedupeKey` and `applyUrl` must not reach `RankableJob`. The exact-fields test checks this.
- **Add both `family` and `row.id` to `seenFamilies`.** A later duplicate of a skipped original points at that original's id.
- **Rows rejected by `keep` must not be remembered,** or they would hide their passing copy.
- **`skipped` counts rows, not postings:** two failing copies count as 2.
